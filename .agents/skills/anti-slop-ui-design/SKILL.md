# Anti-Slop UI & Web Aesthetics Auditor

Use this skill to diagnose and cure generic "AI Slop" website designs.

## 1. AI Slop Red Flags (Kill on Sight)
- **Generic Purple/Indigo Gradients**: AI default colors (e.g. Tailwind `from-purple-600 to-indigo-500` glowing hero blobs).
- **Pointless Floating Glowing Blobs**: Blurred circular background divs that add no context or brand value.
- **Over-Rounded "SaaS Cookie Cutter" Cards**: Every section wrapped in identical rounded-2xl cards with soft grey borders and drop-shadows.
- **Stock Vague Copywriting**: Hero sections with zero personality (e.g. *"Empowering the future of seamless workflows with next-gen AI"*).
- **Hollow Icon Grids**: Grids of 6 cards with random Lucide/Heroicons and 2 sentences of generic filler text.

## 2. Authentic, Professional Design Rules
- **Typography as Primary Architecture**:
  - Distinctive font pairings (e.g. geometric grotesque sans, brutalist mono, or elegant serif for editorial touch).
  - Clear typographic scale (h1 -> h6) with confident font weights and tight tracking for large headers.
- **Deliberate Color Palettes**:
  - High intentional contrast: monochrome with one punchy accent, muted warm retro, crisp stark brutalism, or deep slate minimalism.
  - Never use more than 1 primary accent color unless creating a specialized playful theme.
- **Meaningful Layouts & Density**:
  - Use whitespace to create rhythm, not just empty space.
  - Asymmetric or editorial layouts instead of uniform 3-column card grids.
- **Micro-Interactions That Matter**:
  - Snappy, purposeful transitions (150ms-250ms ease-out). No sluggish floating bounce animations.
  - Keyboard accessible focus rings and crisp hover/active states.
